libjxl 0.11.0 (JPEG XL reference implementation), bundled for PyrA.

Origin: https://github.com/libjxl/libjxl, tag v0.11.0, with its submodules
  third_party/highway  457c891775a7397bdb0376bb1031e6e027af1c48 (Highway 1.2.0)
  third_party/brotli   36533a866ed1ca4b75cf049f4521e4ec5fe24727
  third_party/skcms    42030a771244ba67f86b1c1c76a6493f873c5f91

The files present are unmodified. Removed because PyrA does not build them:
  - .git metadata, testdata/, doc/, examples/, plugins/, debian/, CI and Bazel files
  - the submodules lcms, libpng, zlib, libjpeg-turbo, sjpeg, googletest and
    HEVCSoftware (skcms is the colour engine; no PNG/JPEG tools are built)
  - Highway: g3doc/, docs/, debian/, .github/
  - Brotli: everything but c/, scripts/, CMakeLists.txt, LICENSE and README.md
    (tests, research, and the Java/JS/C#/Go/Python bindings)
  - skcms: everything but skcms.cc, skcms.h, skcms_internal.h, src/, LICENSE
    and README.chromium (tests, profiles, fuzzers, build files)
tools/ stays because libjxl's CMakeLists.txt always adds it.
